## Mortgage Rates Hold Steady Above 6.5%

Mortgage rates barely budged this week, with the 30-year fixed rate ticking up just one basis point to 6.66% as of August 27th. The 15-year fixed rate sits at 5.98%. While this minimal movement might feel like a non-event, the broader story is that we're now firmly settled into what appears to be a new normal for borrowing costs in our Twin Cities market.

## What This Means for Your Monthly Payment

Let's put this in practical terms. On a $450,000 home in the Minneapolis-Saint Paul metro - right around our current median price - a buyer putting 20% down ($90,000) would finance $360,000. At this week's 6.66% rate, that translates to a principal and interest payment of about $2,310 per month.

Compare that to the same scenario at 3% (which we saw as recently as early 2022), and the monthly payment would have been around $1,520. That's a difference of nearly $800 per month, or $9,500 per year. This is the reality our buyers are navigating right now.

## Historical Context: Where Do We Stand?

Here's some perspective that might surprise you: today's rates aren't historically unusual. They're actually close to the long-term average. If you look back over the past 50 years, the 30-year fixed mortgage rate has averaged around 7.75%. We spent most of the 2010s in an extraordinarily low-rate environment that wasn't normal - it was exceptional monetary policy responding to the Great Recession.

The challenge isn't that rates are abnormally high. It's that many buyers in our market got anchored to those ultra-low rates and are now adjusting to what historically qualifies as a moderate borrowing environment. That psychological shift takes time.

## Smart Moves in Today's Market

**For Buyers:** Don't wait for rates to drop dramatically. The inventory situation in the Twin Cities remains tight, and if rates do decrease significantly, you'll face more competition and likely higher prices. Remember, you can refinance your rate later if conditions improve, but you can't refinance the purchase price you pay today.

Focus on buying the right home at a fair price. Work with your lender to understand all your financing options - including adjustable-rate mortgages if you don't plan to stay long-term, or buydown programs that can reduce your effective rate.

**For Sellers:** This rate environment has actually created opportunity. Serious buyers are still out there, but they're more selective. Make sure your home is priced correctly from day one, and consider that presentation matters more than ever. Buyers are doing their homework and won't overpay for a property that needs significant work.

We're also seeing creative deal structures work well - seller credits toward closing costs or rate buydowns can make your property stand out without actually reducing your net proceeds.
