## This Week's Rate Movement

Mortgage rates edged higher this week, with the 30-year fixed rate climbing to 6.66% as of July 30th - up from 6.58% the previous week. That's an 8 basis point increase, and while it might seem small, it matters when you're financing a home purchase in our market.

The 15-year fixed rate now sits at 6.04%, which remains an attractive option for buyers with larger down payments or those looking to build equity faster.

## What This Means for Your Monthly Payment

Let's make this concrete. On a $450,000 home purchase with 20% down (a $360,000 loan), here's how this week's rate increase affects your monthly principal and interest payment:

- At 6.58%: $2,286 per month
- At 6.66%: $2,304 per month

That's an $18 monthly difference, or about $216 per year. Not catastrophic, but it adds up over the life of the loan. More importantly, it's a reminder that rates can move quickly, and waiting for the "perfect" rate could cost you more than locking in today's numbers.

## Historical Context: Where Do We Stand?

Here's the perspective we're sharing with our clients: yes, 6.66% is significantly higher than the historic lows of 2020-2021 when rates dipped below 3%. But it's important to remember that those were the anomaly, not the norm.

Looking at a longer timeline, rates in the mid-6% range are roughly average for the past few decades. Our parents and grandparents bought homes at 8%, 10%, even 14% rates during certain periods. The difference is that home prices were lower then - today's buyers face the double challenge of elevated rates **and** elevated prices.

The silver lining? When rates do eventually come down, we'll see refinancing opportunities. You can't refinance the purchase price, but you can refinance your rate.

## Tactical Advice for Right Now

**For Buyers:** Don't let rate anxiety paralyze you. If you've found the right home in the right location at a price that works for your budget, these rates are workable. Consider asking sellers about rate buydowns or other concessions in your offer. We're seeing this strategy gain traction in our market, especially on properties that have been listed longer than 30 days.

Also, get creative with financing. The 15-year option at 6.04% could save you substantial interest if your monthly budget allows for the higher payment.

**For Sellers:** Understand that buyers are more payment-sensitive than ever. Pricing your home competitively from day one is critical. Overpriced listings are sitting longer in this rate environment. We're also seeing success with sellers offering to cover closing costs or contributing to rate buydowns - it helps your listing stand out and makes the math work better for buyers.
