### Fair housing (federal)

- 42 U.S.C. 3604(c) bars statements/ads indicating a preference based on a protected
  class. HUD removed its 1995 "Achtenberg" advertising guidance from its site in 2025, but
  the statute (and Minn. Stat. 363A.09) still apply, and that memo is still the best list of
  risky words ("safe", "family neighborhood", "exclusive", "walking distance to [church]").
- The Equal Housing Opportunity logo/statement isn't mandated for an agent website by
  statute; HUD's poster rule (24 CFR Part 110) applies to offices. It's standard practice
  and likely a CB brand requirement. `ListingDisclosure` now draws an EHO house mark with
  the words "Equal Housing Opportunity" (**FIXED**: the old text box read "EHO");
  TODO-JIM: use HUD's official artwork if the broker wants the exact mark.
- Close to Home counts places only; it doesn't rate neighborhoods or describe residents. Keep
  it that way (no crime scores or school ratings without fair-housing review).

### Accessibility (ADA / WCAG)

- No federal regulation sets a standard for private websites under ADA Title III; DOJ's
  2022 guidance treats public-facing sites of public accommodations as covered, and courts
  disagree on websites without a physical location. The Minnesota Human Rights Act
  (363A.11) also covers public accommodations. WCAG 2.2 AA is the practical target.
- New `/accessibility` page states the goal, current measures and known limits (maps,
  photo alt text), with a contact.
- Gaps seen while auditing (not fixed; not my files): no "skip to content" link
  (`Layout.astro:88`); `text-stone-400` on white is ~2.5:1 contrast and used for labels in
  listing sections (`ListedBy.astro:17`, `Hero.astro`, `FinancialDetails.astro`): WCAG AA
  needs 4.5:1 for small text, so use `text-stone-500` or darker; the Mapbox map and draw
  tool aren't keyboard/screen-reader usable (list view is the alternative); the typing
  indicator and photo slide-in animations ignore reduced motion. Run axe/Lighthouse on the
  main pages before go-live.

## EULA options

**Rule (§5.8(j)):** once per viewing session, the visitor must assent to NorthstarMLS's
EULA **by an affirmative act before viewing any MLS data**; adopt new EULA versions within
30 days.

**Is the text obtainable?** Yes. It's NorthstarMLS's "End-User License Agreement for
consumers accessing public MLS and broker web sites" (six sections: per-session access,
title, personal non-commercial license, limited use incl. no scraping, revocation, general
terms incl. $500 liability cap and Minnesota law). Many Minnesota broker sites host copies,
but we must use the **current official version**: request it from NorthstarMLS when giving
the §5.8(g) notice, and subscribe to updates (30-day adoption clock).

**What NorthstarMLS has accepted before:** the previous rules (§12.5, eff. 2025-01-01)
had the same wording plus a note: assent "can be done unobtrusively by putting a link above
the 'Search' button ... 'I have read and agree to the terms of the license agreement' ...
The affirmative act requirement is fulfilled when the consumer clicks the search button."
The 2026 rules dropped the note but kept the rule.

## Audit trail

**§5.9(b):** "maintain an audit trail of each user's activities for at least 18 months."
Being designed API-side by another agent. Interpretation for that design:

- "User" here is the platform's visitor (and, later, registrant). Without accounts, a
  pseudonymous id per browser session (random, cookie-held) plus the request id is
  reasonable; keep IP addresses truncated/hashed (CLAUDE.md: never full IPs in logs).
- Log what NorthstarMLS would ask for under §6.15 (records within 7 days of a request):
  EULA assent (time, version), searches (criteria, count returned), listing views (listing
  key), photo/lightbox bulk access, chat use (listing key, message count; **not content**,
  per CLAUDE.md), contact requests sent (no content), and rate-limit/blocked events.
- Retention ≥18 months in a store with export (Cloud Logging's default 30 days isn't
  enough: route to a log bucket with 18+ month retention or BigQuery).
- Update `/privacy` to disclose the log and its retention.
